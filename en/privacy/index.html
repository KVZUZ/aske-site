Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

ASKE (“we”, “our”) takes the protection of your personal information seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who it is shared with and how we protect it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email address and password (stored hashed by Supabase Auth, never in plain text). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name and profile picture from Google.
  • Profile: first name, last name, sex, date of birth, weight, height, language, units, training goal, level and sports practised.
  • Body measurements (optional): weight history and body measurements, if you enter them.
  • Training data: sessions, exercises, sets, reps, loads, programmes, records, notes, runs and rides, with their metrics (distance, duration, pace, power, cadence, elevation, heart rate).
  • GPS routes: the route of the activities you import, when they have one. The app does not access your device’s location.
  • Health data (optional): with your explicit consent, ASKE reads from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) your workouts, their routes, your heart rate (including resting), distances, steps, calories, power, speed and VO2 max. ASKE only reads this data and never writes to Health Connect or Apple Health.
  • Strava data (optional): if you connect Strava, your athlete ID, your Strava access tokens and the imported activities (metrics, heart rate and GPS routes).
  • Imported files (optional): the FIT, GPX or TCX activity files (or their archives) you open in ASKE.
  • Subscription: Premium status, access end date and the transaction ID of purchases made in the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details.
  • Push notifications: an Expo Push token stored on our servers only if you allow notifications.
  • Crash and performance reports: when the app crashes or hits an error, technical information (device model and OS, app version, error trace, screens visited just before) linked to your account’s internal ID, without screenshots.
  • App usage statistics: screens opened (as page types, without the content you enter), app opening and installation, device model and OS, app version. These statistics are anonymous: they are not linked to your account, only to a random identifier specific to the installation. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > About > Usage statistics. No health or training data is included.
  • Waitlist (website): email address, language and interest in the Founder offer, if you sign up on aske.fit.

To create an account, only your email address, first name, sex and at least one sport practised are required. All other data is optional: without it, some features are simply not available.

2. How we use data

Each use relies on a legal basis, shown in brackets.

  • To provide the app’s features: session tracking, programmes, statistics and records (performance of the contract).
  • To back up your data on our servers and sync it between your devices when you are signed in (performance of the contract).
  • To import your activities from Health Connect, Apple Health, Strava or files, at your request, automatically in the background for Premium users (explicit consent).
  • To send training reminders if you enable notifications (consent).
  • To manage your subscription and check your Premium status (performance of the contract).
  • To fix crashes and improve the app’s stability (legitimate interest in providing a reliable app).
  • To understand which features are used and improve the app (legitimate interest in improving the app; anonymous statistics, which can be turned off in the settings).
  • To email you when the app launches if you joined the waitlist (consent).

We never sell, rent or share your data for advertising. Health data is only used to show your history and statistics in ASKE: it is never used for advertising, profiling or training artificial intelligence models. ASKE makes no automated decisions about you: the statistics and recommendations computed in the app have no effect outside of it.

3. Storage, security and transfers

Your data is stored locally on your device. When you are signed in to your account, it is also backed up on Supabase servers hosted by Amazon Web Services in Canada, where each account can only access its own data. Communications are encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.

Our service providers (section 8) may process your data outside Québec and Canada, notably in the United States and the European Union. We only use providers that offer adequate protection of your information. Please note, however, that wherever your data is processed, it is subject to local laws: US authorities can demand data directly from a US provider, without going through us.

4. Requests from authorities

We only disclose your data to a public authority when the law requires it, upon a valid order from a court of competent jurisdiction. We challenge requests that are overbroad or lack a clear legal basis. We notify you of a request before responding to it, unless the law or the court forbids it, or in an emergency involving a person’s life or safety. We do not take part in any bulk data collection or surveillance programme.

5. Data retention

Your data is kept as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all related data at any time from the app settings or by emailing us (see Delete my account). Deletion also revokes ASKE’s access to your Strava account. Only the transaction ID of a Founder purchase is kept, detached from your account, as proof of purchase. Waitlist signups are deleted on request.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase and port your data, to object to its processing and to withdraw your consent, notably under Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector and under the GDPR if you live in the European Union. You can export your data from the app settings, and revoke ASKE’s access to Health Connect, Apple Health or Strava at any time from their own settings. To exercise your other rights, email us at privacy@aske.fit. You can also file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

7. Children

ASKE is not intended for people under 14. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Third-party services

  • Supabase: data hosting, authentication and waitlist.
  • Google: Sign in with Google, optional, and Health Connect on Android.
  • Apple: Apple Health on iOS, optional.
  • Strava: activity import, optional.
  • RevenueCat: subscription and purchase management.
  • Sentry: crash and performance reports, hosted in the European Union.
  • PostHog: app usage statistics, hosted in the European Union.
  • Expo / EAS: push notifications and app updates.
  • OpenFreeMap: base maps shown under your routes; your device downloads map tiles directly from their servers, which therefore see your IP address.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If a significant change is made, we will let you know through the app or by email. The update date at the top of the page shows the version in force.

10. Contact

ASKE’s founder is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. For any question or request: privacy@aske.fit.
