Delete my account

Last updated: October 26, 2026

You can delete your ASKE account and all related data at any time, in two ways.

From the app

  1. Open ASKE and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

By email

If you no longer have access to the app, write to privacy@aske.fit from your account’s email address, with the subject “Account deletion”. We handle the request within 30 days and confirm the deletion by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile.
  • All your training data: sessions, programmes, records and metrics.
  • Your connections to third-party services (Strava).

An active Premium subscription is cancelled separately, in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
